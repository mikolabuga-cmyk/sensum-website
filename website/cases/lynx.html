The key question

Will marketers hand the routine of a campaign to AI agents, and how do they want to talk to them?

What we decided

A working prototype, six marketers. Three decisions, one reversal.

01

No agent to pick. All four work behind one conversation.

We assumed marketers would choose the right agent for each task. In testing they skipped the list and typed straight into the prompt, expecting output right away.

Our first idea, rejected
02

Chat at the centre. UI only where it adds clarity.

People relied on the prompt and found extra controls slowing them down.

03

Guided fields for every brief, inside the conversation.

Open-ended questions left people unsure what to enter. Guided choices made briefs faster and the result easier to trust.

What shipped

Four agents under the surface. One workspace on top.

Marketers brief a campaign in plain language. Research, Plan, Create and Analyze agents do the routine work, grounded in the company's own files.

Shipped in 3 months. Adopted in 7 countries.
