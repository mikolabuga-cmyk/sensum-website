The key question

What would make people finish the calculator, and share their contacts willingly?

Where it started

One calculator. Seven questions before any answer.

A number came only after name, dates, style and contacts. People spent 1:45 on it, called it irritating, and 64% were on a phone.

What we decided

Ask only what the estimate needs. Give something back for every contact.

KPIs first, screens second. AI turned our sketches into a clickable prototype we tested with users within the week.

Trade-off
